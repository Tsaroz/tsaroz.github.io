About NepalRanks

NepalRanks is a community ranking platform for Nepal built on one simple conviction: rankings of things people use every day, banks, internet providers, universities, cities, restaurants, should be decided by the people who use them, not by an editorial desk or the highest bidder.

What we do

The community votes on named rankings such as Best Banks in Nepal or Best Momo Spots in Kathmandu. Every entry carries an upvote score, a downvote score and a net score, and the net score alone determines the position. Discussion travels with each entry, so voters can always see why something sits where it sits.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell positions, boost rankings or accept payment for placement.
  • Administrators create categories and keep discussions civil, they cannot move an entry.
  • There is no editorial ranking. If a position looks wrong, the fix is more voters, not a press release.

Where the project stands

NepalRanks is a live platform: every vote is counted the second it is cast, every comment appears instantly, and page order moves with the scores. Rankings are seeded by the team and grow with the community, city by city. Google sign-in guards voting and commenting, your nickname is public, your email stays between you and the moderation team, and community-submitted rankings are next.

The name and the promise

Our tagline is Your Voice. Your Rankings. The platform exists to make that promise mechanically true: your vote counts exactly as much as anyone else's, and every ranking movement is visible in the numbers.
